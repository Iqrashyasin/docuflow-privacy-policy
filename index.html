Privacy Policy

Information about privacy when using DocuFlow.

This policy describes DocuFlow's file-processing approach and its use of Google AdMob advertising. Some details depend on the app version, device, and advertising settings.

1. Introduction

DocuFlow is an Android application that provides tools for working with PDF files and other supported file formats. This Privacy Policy explains relevant privacy considerations when you use the application.

2. File Processing

DocuFlow's file-processing features are designed to process files locally on your device. Files handled by these local features are processed on the device rather than being uploaded to a DocuFlow server for that processing.

This statement applies to local file-processing functionality. It does not establish that every component of the app, including advertising or any future online feature, operates offline. The behavior of individual features may vary.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

DocuFlow uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, and advertising-related data, depending on the SDK configuration, your device, and your privacy choices.

Advertising data may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where permitted, personalized advertising. DocuFlow does not independently control all information collected by Google through its advertising SDK.

Learn more about Google's practices: Google Privacy Policy.

You can also review Google's guidance about the AdMob SDK .

4. Information and Permissions

Information accessed by DocuFlow depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant, such as access to files or images when required for a selected task.

The exact categories of information collected or shared by the app and its third-party SDKs depend on the implemented functionality and configuration. Please review the app's Google Play Data safety section for the applicable disclosures.

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Google AdMob may process information as described in Google's privacy documentation. Other data handling, if any, depends on the app's enabled features and integrated services. This policy does not claim that no information is ever collected or shared.

6. Data Retention and Security

Locally processed files may remain on your device according to your actions and the app's storage behavior. Retention practices for advertising-related information are governed in part by the relevant service provider's policies.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

DocuFlow is not intended to be directed at children where prohibited by applicable law. If you believe personal information has been handled improperly in relation to a child, seek assistance through the app's publisher contact details once provided.

8. Your Choices

You can manage available device privacy settings and advertising preferences where supported. You can also choose which files to open or process using the app. Available controls may vary by device, region, and application version.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features, advertising configuration, or applicable requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the app's publisher using the support contact provided on the DocuFlow Google Play Store listing.

A dedicated privacy contact email has not yet been provided. Add a monitored contact address before publishing this policy.
